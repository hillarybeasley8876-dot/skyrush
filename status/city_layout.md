# city_layout status

Producer of `data/city/city.json` (the playable map). Everything is generated by
`tools/city/build_city.py` — the JSON is never hand-edited.

## Done

**Tools (all under `tools/city/`, pure Python 3.9 stdlib except the renderer):**

| file | what |
|---|---|
| `citylib.py` | json io, uniform Catmull-Rom (the same formula as `blender/lib/geo.py`), resample, road width, edge polylines, polygon helpers, a spatial index |
| `city_geom.py` | arcs, spirals, Hermite blends, fillets, sweeps, monotone cubic |
| `city_profile.py` | the **grade profile**: constant-grade tangents joined by parabolic vertical curves (L ≥ 2000·Δg), the solver that keeps the grade and the kink limit |
| `city_road.py` | the `Road` record: plan + height anchors -> 2 m control points, the lane centres and headings, the attach handling |
| `city_roads.py` / `city_roads_east.py` | every named road of the dossier §10.3 (Yan'an elevated, N–S, Inner Ring, Bund, Zhongshan E-2, Century Ave, Lujiazui Ring, Pudong South, the Yan'an E. Rd tunnel, the Nanpu spiral / bridge / Pudong loop, the G50, the frontage roads, the tunnel portal link) |
| `dragon.py` | the dragon-pillar interchange: 4 left-turn ramps (L3 16.5 m, L4 23.9 m) and 4 right-turn ramps |
| `city_ramps.py` | the ramp pairs between the Yan'an elevated and Xizang Rd / Chongqing Rd (a geometry search: fillet first, sweep as the fallback) |
| `city_grid.py` | the surface network: the 14 districts with their own grain, the named Puxi streets, the lattice, the creek bridges |
| `city_snap.py` | the street ends are snapped onto the road they meet: on the centre-line of a road they cross, at the edge of a road they run along; a short link road joins the rest |
| `city_blocks.py` | the 10 m raster -> one lot per free cell, styled per district, the declared strips (Bund frontage / promenade / Century Ave green strips) cut at the cross streets, the POI sites |
| `city_landmarks.py` | the dossier anchors (the Bund row, the three Lujiazui towers, the Pearl Ring, the dragon pillar, the Nanpu towers) as `plaza` lots with their real heights, moved clear of the roads where they need it |
| `city_marks.py` | POIs (toll, 2 gas, 3 charge, 2 police, 4 hiding, 4 roadblock, 14 landmark), 5 sky lanes, 5 race routes, the spawns |
| `city_junctions.py` | the junctions of the surface network (crossings and T junctions, found with a spatial index) |
| `build_city.py` | builds and writes `data/city/city.json` (deterministic) |
| `validate.py` | CLI: 16 rules, one PASS/FAIL line each with **every** failure listed, exits non-zero |
| `city_rules.py` | the road rules: grades, kinks, deck clearance, the water, the attaches, the junctions, the key levels, the dead ends |
| `city_rules_lanes.py` / `city_rules_graph.py` / `city_rules_routes.py` / `city_rules_map.py` | the lane centres and the directed lane graph, the race routes and the sky lanes, the blocks, the decks, the spawns, the POIs and the land coverage |
| `render_map.py` | `captures/city_layout/map.png` (2400 px, north up) and `profiles.png` |

## Requests (to core / other units)

* **landmarks unit** — the dragon-pillar geometry is still physically
  impossible as specified: the brief asks for left-turn ramps that pass ≤ 3 m
  from the pillar axis at Y = 16.5 m and Y = 23.9 m, while the pillar
  (Ø 5.0 m) runs from Y = 0 to Y = 29.4 m.  The L3/L4/L5 decks cross the
  pillar axis at 16.5 / 23.9 / 31.0 m, so the pillar asset must stop below
  Y ≈ 14.9 m or carry the decks on portal frames.  It is flagged in the JSON
  `notes`.
* **roads unit** — the JSON stores the centrelines as 2 m control points
  (~57 000 points in total).  Please sample the Catmull-Rom from them as
  usual; do not treat them as sparse control points.  The `heading_deg` of a
  3D tangent is the heading of the road (0 = north, 90 = east).
* **world unit** — `spawns.police` are on the lane centres of the road next to
  the two police stations; the traffic density is 1.0.
