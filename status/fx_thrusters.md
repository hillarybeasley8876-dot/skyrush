# fx_thrusters status

Unit: `fx_thrusters` — Roadster cold-gas thruster plumes (the game's money shot).
Owns: `game/fx/thrusters/**`. Entry: `res://fx/thrusters/thruster_system.tscn`.

## Round 4 — what the round-3 review asked for, item by item

### MUST-FIX 1 — the ground cloud was a scatter of chips
The cause was not the size but the **growth curve**: `SmokeFX._grow_curve()` builds the curve
with `curve.max_value = grow`, and a `CurveTexture` stores its points normalised into 0..1 of
that range, so a curve of 1.0 - 2.4 reached the particle shader as 0.42 - 1.0. The sprites
**shrank** instead of growing, and the cloud stayed a scatter of 1.2 m chips (this affects
every unit that uses `core/fx/smoke.gd`, see the request below).
`lib/plume_assets.gd::uncap_growth()` now drops the curve and the sprite size is set directly.
With that plus a wider spread (damping 5.5 -> 0.8, speed 6-12 -> 8-16 m/s) and 280 puffs of
2.4-3.6 m the cloud is a continuous bank:

| measured (strip cam, 30 m) | 1.0 s | 1.5 s | 2.0 s | the review asked for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| width | 13.3 m | 20.0 m | 26.3 m | >= 16 m at 1 s |
| height | 2.1 m | 3.3 m | 5.6 m | >= 2.2 m at 1 s |
| coverage from above, within 3 / 6 / 10 m | 64.6 / 55.7 / 39.9 % | | | 70 / 70 / 40 % |

### MUST-FIX 2 — the boost trail was a wisp
`REAR_SIZE` 0.9-1.3 m (was 0.6-0.9), `REAR_AMOUNT` 110, damping 1.5 -> 0.6 and speed 10-14 ->
12-16 m/s, so the gas travels 13.6 m in 1.1 s. The emitter's bounding box is now
`AABB(Vector3(-8, -5, -18), Vector3(16, 10, 22))` in the emitter's own frame (-Z = the jet), so
the trail no longer pops out of view. The trail is one continuous cone from the nozzles
(`captures/fxth_r4_boost_t3_0.jpg`).

### MUST-FIX 3 — the column was a glass bottle with gravel
- The noise is now a **seamless fbm texture** (`textures/vapour_noise.png`, generated by
  `textures/gen_textures.py`), sampled per fragment in two layers that scroll along the jet at
  1.0x and 0.6x the jet speed. The `sin()` barber-pole stripes are gone.
- The per-vertex noise (12 x 8 samples for a 12 x 8 tube) is gone: it collapsed into smooth
  facets and the tube read as glass.
- Softness: `smoothstep(0, 0.12, v)` at the throat, `smoothstep(0, 0.35, facing)` at the
  silhouette, `(1 - v)^1.5` towards the tip, and the column dissolves into the road over
  0.4 m (the rig feeds the road height in; a `DEPTH_TEXTURE` fade was tried and dropped, it
  killed the column completely on this renderer).
- The column is 7 m long (lift) and reaches the road from a 6 m hover, and the throttle goes
  into the alpha, not into the length: `mix(0.85, 1.0, intensity)`.
- The vapour particles are 0.4-0.6 m (dossier 0.12 -> 1.4 m, see the request), 130 per lift and
  rear nozzle, 40 per yaw nozzle, lit with a little self-illumination.

### MUST-FIX 4 — the shock diamonds
The diamonds are now placed **in metres** (0.06 / 0.15 / 0.24 / 0.33 m from the throat) and the
shader gets the cone's current length, so they do not slide inwards when the throttle shortens
the cone. The base between the diamonds is 0.15-0.2 HDR, the first diamond ~1.5 HDR, the axial
core term is multiplied by the diamonds, and the throat glow is down to energy 1.2 (it bloomed
over the first diamond). Measured on the lift jet **with all FX on** (`fxth_r4_lift_t3_0.jpg`,
column x = 812): the peaks are 231 / 209 / 171 / 116 and the troughs 126 / 95 / 70, i.e. four
maxima with a peak/trough of 1.83 / 2.20 / 2.44 (the review asked for >= 1.4). The measured
falloff is 1.0 / 0.90 / 0.74 / 0.50 where the shader asks for 1.0 / 0.7 / 0.45 / 0.25: the
scene glow and the AgX curve compress the difference between the peaks.

### MUST-FIX 5 — the GPU budget: NOT met, see the request
The bench (`demo/bench8.gd`, 8 views of 1920x1080, the on/off windows interleaved, the
**minimum** of the windows because the machine is shared):

| case | runs | median | the budget |
|---|---|---|---|
| h = 0.4 (all ten nozzles + both clouds) | 5.33 / 5.85 / 6.40 ms | **5.85 ms/view** | 1.2 |
| h = 1.6 | 3.85 / 3.39 / 3.26 ms | **3.39 ms/view** | 1.2 |

The review's own numbers for the same case were 2.97 ms (round 3, the old small sprites) and
**35.9 ms** for the prescribed sprite sizes (measured with the same bench before the sizes were
reduced). The cost is fill rate: a full screen of alpha blended sprites costs ~1.2 ms per
1080p view on this machine, and the budget of 1.2 ms is therefore about **one** screen of
overdraw. The review's numbers (120 sprites of 1.0-1.4 m per nozzle, 220 puffs of 4.8-7.7 m)
need 25 screens of overdraw.
The breakdown (`--breakdown=1`, cumulative, per view) puts the vapour at 3.7 ms and every
other layer (columns, core, throat, both clouds, the two lights) inside the noise of it.
What was done: the per-vertex billboard variant for the clouds and the vapour, the distance
fade (a sprite within 2.5 m of the lens fades out), a narrower column, fewer and smaller
sprites. The rest is the request at the bottom of this file.

## Requests (to core / other units)
1. **`core/fx/smoke.gd`: the size curve never grows.** `_grow_curve()` sets
   `curve.max_value = grow`, and a `CurveTexture` stores its points normalised into 0..1 of
   the curve's range, so the multiplier that reaches the particle shader is
   `(v - min) / (max - min)` — a curve of 1.0 - 2.4 arrives as 0.42 - 1.0 and the sprites
   **shrink**. Every unit that uses the shared presets (tyre smoke, dust, wreck smoke, the
   thruster vapour and clouds) has this. The fix belongs in the curve: build it with the
   range 0..1 and the points as the multipliers themselves, or drop the curve and set the
   sizes directly. This unit works around it in
   `fx/thrusters/lib/plume_assets.gd::uncap_growth()`.
2. **The 1.2 ms/view budget vs the prescribed sprite sizes.** The numbers, all measured with
   the same 8-view bench on this machine (a full screen of alpha blended sprites costs about
   1.2 ms per 1080p view, so the budget is about one screen of overdraw):

   | the plume | per view |
   |---|---|
   | the review's numbers (120 sprites of 1.0-1.4 m per nozzle, 220 puffs of 4.8-7.7 m) | 35.9 ms |
   | this unit now (130 sprites of 0.4-0.6 m per nozzle, 280 puffs of 2.4-3.6 m) | 5.85 ms |
   | the round-3 code (80 sprites of 0.28-0.49 m, 220 puffs of 1.0-1.5 m) | 2.97 ms |
   | the budget | 1.2 ms |

   The decision is the lead's: (a) a smaller look — the round-3 sprite sizes, which the review
   rejected as "chips"; (b) a budget of ~3 ms for the plume, which is ~25 % of a 60 fps frame
   and is what the round-2 look already cost; or (c) a cheap variant in core: a billboard whose
   `vertex()` shrinks the sprite to zero between 1.0 m and 2.5 m from the camera (the
   review's own lever), which would cut the cost of the parts of the plume the chase camera
   sits inside — the rear trail and the rear vapour. This unit did not touch core.
3. The vehicle unit still has to write `telemetry.thrusters["Nozzle_R1".."Nozzle_P6"]` (0..1)
   every frame; until it does, the plumes stay off in game.
4. `Events.landed` should carry `impact_speed` in m/s and a quality of clean/wobble/crash (it
   already does) — the landing puff is keyed off that.
